## Description

Go deeper into God's Word. Bible in Context is a free, in-depth Bible study app that surrounds every verse with the tools serious readers want — cross-references, the original Greek and Hebrew, and trusted classic commentary — all bundled offline and completely free.

**Five trusted translations, all offline:**
• Berean Standard Bible (BSB) — modern, accurate, and easy to read
• World English Bible (WEB) — clear contemporary English
• King James Version (KJV) — the timeless classic
• American Standard Version (ASV) — formal and precise
• Young's Literal Translation (YLT) — word-for-word literal

Every translation is in the public domain, so the full text lives on your device — no internet required.

**Study every verse in context:**
• Cross-references that connect Scripture to Scripture
• Greek & Hebrew word studies with Strong's lexicon
• Verse-by-verse commentary from Matthew Henry, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown, and Tyndale
• Plain-English explanations of difficult passages

**Make it yours:**
• Highlight verses and write private or shareable notes
• Bookmark passages and keep a prayer journal
• Follow reading plans — or create your own
• A fresh Verse of the Day, every day

**Study together:**
• Follow other readers and share highlights and notes
• Keep anything private with a single tap

**Built on principle:**
• No AI-generated content — insights come straight from the lexicon and public-domain commentaries
• No external calls for Scripture — everything is bundled and works fully offline
• Optional account — the whole app works without signing in; sign in only to sync across devices
• Free. No ads. No subscriptions.

Download Bible in Context and read the Bible the way it was meant to be studied — in context.
