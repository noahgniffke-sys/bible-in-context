## Description

Bible in Context is a free, offline-first Bible study app. Five public-domain translations are bundled in the app, so you can read with no internet:

• Berean Standard Bible (BSB) — modern and easy to read. This is the default.
• World English Bible (WEB)
• King James Version (KJV)
• American Standard Version (ASV)
• Young's Literal Translation (YLT)

The verse of the day is shown in the translation you are reading. A reminder at noon brings that verse while the app is closed.

Search finds John 3:16, a whole verse, or a sentence even when a few words are wrong.

Every verse opens into a context view. Tap any verse to see:

• Cross-references — passages that quote, echo, or sit beside this one (Treasury of Scripture Knowledge)
• Greek or Hebrew with Strong's lexicon — tap any word for its meaning, its grammar, and other verses that use it
• A plain-English line for what the verse is saying
• Verse type and the context of the passage
• Study notes from Tyndale, Jamieson-Fausset-Brown, and John Gill, written out in today's English
• A short summary of the chapter

Personal study tools:

• Highlight verses in six colors
• Save notes. They are private unless you choose to share one.
• Bookmark up to five verses
• A prayer journal that marks prayers as answered

Community:

• Follow other readers. Follows stay in sync on every device signed into the same account.
• See public highlights and notes from people you follow
• Read the built-in plans, or sign in to create and share your own
• Search people by username or name

Six themes. Pick one on Home or Profile:

• Noah theme (dark) — cathedral stone, leather, parchment, blood red, and gold
• Allie theme (light) — cream parchment, pastoral green, royal gold, and soft pink
• Japan — cream, vermillion, and sumi black
• Wedding — white with baby blue and baby yellow
• Blue Night — black and blue
• Nathan theme — Charleston green, olive, and midnight blue

Sign in with Apple or Google to open the app. If you are already signed in, you go straight in and stay signed in. Your notes, highlights, bookmarks, prayers, follows, and plan progress stay with that account. After you sign in, reading still works offline.

The study notes come from public-domain, Bible-believing scholars. Scripture is the final authority.

Completely free. No ads. No subscriptions. No tracking.
